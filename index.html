Instructions

Enter one account line at a time in Journal Entry Input. Give all lines of a transaction the same Entry ID. A transaction appears in reports only after every line is valid and its debits equal its credits.

  1. Add accounts to the Chart of Accounts if needed.
  2. Enter opening balances and transactions as balanced Regular entries. To distinguish beginning capital from later contributions, enter opening capital with a description containing Opening capital.
  3. Enter adjustments in the Adjusting Entries sheet or select Adjusting in the main input. Supply actual adjustment amounts; none are estimated.
  4. Review the worksheet, statements, generated closing entries, and post-closing balance.
  5. For eligible accrual adjustments only, mark every line of that entry Reverse. The exact opposite posts on the first day after the period end.

This is a spreadsheet-format workspace, not a downloadable native spreadsheet file. Calculated columns and schedules update from saved source lines. Current-period statements include valid Regular and Adjusting entries dated on or before the period end. Closing is generated from those balances. Reversals are displayed as next-period postings, not included in current-period statements.
